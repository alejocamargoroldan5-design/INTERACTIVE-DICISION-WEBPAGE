Interactive Decision

Build a decision problem under uncertainty, edit the payoffs and probabilities, and watch four criteria and a staged filter pick the best alternative in real time.

MaximaxMaximinMinimax RegretEMV / Bayes

1Choose an example

Load a preset problem, or start blank and enter your own. Every example can be edited afterwards.

2Decision model

Payoffs are benefits (higher is better). Rename anything by typing in the dark header cells or first column. Probabilities are in percent.

3Criteria results

Each criterion recommends an alternative. Ties are shown when two or more alternatives share the best value.

Comparison table

Show regret matrix (best payoff in each state minus the payoff)

4Staged filtering and flow diagram

Alternatives pass through four screens in order. Each stage removes options and the survivors move on.

Share of the payoff range an alternative may fall short by and still pass stages 2 and 3. 0% keeps only exact ties.
